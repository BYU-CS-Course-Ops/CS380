## Before class

**Read both of these before you come — we'll build on them right away:**

1. **Creative Problem Solving handbook** — the assigned excerpt (posted on the LMS / in course resources). Focus on the *divergent vs. convergent thinking* section and the tool write-ups; you can skim the parts about facilitated group sessions.
2. **"Channeling Your Creativity,"** Elder Robert D. Hales (*New Era*, Feb 2004).

**Also bring:** your idea journal — with a week's worth of entries — and a handful of raw problems you've noticed.

---

## Purpose

This is our first real workshop, and it's mostly *doing.* You'll practice the Creative Problem Solving process hands-on with a quick warm-up exercise, then turn it loose on the real task: **generating a big, wide pool of problems and needs worth founding a project on.** You'll defer judgment, go for quantity, and pour it all into your idea journal — this is the raw material for your Idea Briefs.

---

## Learning objectives

By the end of this session, you'll be able to:

1. **Describe the four stages of Creative Problem Solving** and apply divergent, then convergent, thinking to a problem.
2. **Practice deferring judgment** — generate many ideas before evaluating any of them.
3. **Use several divergent tools** (Post-It brainstorming, brainwriting, and the ladder of abstraction) to explore a problem space.
4. **Generate a broad set of candidate problems** framed around real people and their needs — *not* solutions.
5. **Leave with a much bigger idea journal** and a shortlist of problem spaces to investigate.

---

## What we'll do together

A quick recap and journal check-in; a short consolidation of the CPS process from the reading; a discussion of the Hales article; a hands-on warm-up exercise; and then the main event — a fast, wide problem-finding workshop using the tools above, capturing everything into your journals.

---

## What you'll leave with

- **A materially expanded idea journal** — a lot more candidate problems than you walked in with.
- **3–5 "starred" problem spaces** that tug at you, to investigate further (not a commitment — just a shortlist).
- **Before next class (Mon, Sep 14 — Week 2):** keep feeding your journal and bring your starred problems. Week 2 turns to open source, licensing, and **discovery** — how to talk to real users and understand their needs.
