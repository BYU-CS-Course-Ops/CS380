## Purpose

You've spent three weeks *diverging* — generating and investigating problems. Today you **converge**, and the discipline is to do it *deliberately* — scoring your ideas against a shared yardstick and pressure-testing them with peers — rather than defaulting to whichever idea you're most attached to. By the end you'll have **one or two ideas you can defend**, with a written rationale for each, ready to carry into your **Product Definition Brief** and, next week, your written proposal. Converging isn't marrying an idea; it's choosing a working direction you can defend and revise.

---

## Learning objectives

By the end of this session, you'll be able to:

1. **Apply convergent-thinking principles** — decide deliberately, improve promising ideas rather than only killing weak ones, and avoid premature convergence.
2. **Apply a richness test** — judge whether an idea is *worth founding on*, not just whether you can build it.
3. **Evaluate ideas against explicit criteria** — a portable skill — using this course's selection criteria, and **plot candidates on Value vs. Do-ability.**
4. **Give and receive structured peer feedback** that challenges value, feasibility, and richness.
5. **Choose 1–2 ideas** to carry forward and **write a defensible one-line rationale** for each.
6. **Map your chosen idea onto the Product Definition Brief** (users, needs, success criteria).

## What we'll do together

A **portable way to evaluate any idea** — decide what "good" means, score against those criteria, weigh value vs. do-ability, get feedback, and commit — pointed today at this course's **selection criteria** and the **richness test** (feasible isn't the same as worth founding on), and walked through live on two example ideas so you see the moves before you make them. Then a hands-on workshop on the **Idea Scoring Sheet** (provided): score your own candidates and plot them on **Value vs. Do-ability**, run a **structured peer-feedback round** in small groups, and **commit to one or two** ideas with a written rationale. We close by mapping your chosen idea onto **CP2**.
