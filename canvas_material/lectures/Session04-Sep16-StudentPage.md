## Purpose

You've generated a big pile of candidate problems — and right now, most of them are guesses. Today is about **discovery:** how to talk to real people about their problems *without hearing only what you want to hear*, how to ask non-leading questions, and — when you can't reach a real user — how to build an **honest persona** from real evidence. It's a workshop: you'll practice interviewing in class, then take it to real people this week.

---

## Learning objectives

By the end of this session, you'll be able to:

1. **Explain why naive user questions mislead**, and apply the three Mom Test rules.
2. **Ask open, non-leading questions** and tell a good question from a leading one.
3. **Run a short discovery interview** and capture the needs you hear.
4. **Build an honest, evidence-tagged persona** when real access is limited.
5. **Follow basic consent/ethics** when talking to real people.

---

## What we'll do together

The validation trap and the three Mom Test rules; good vs. leading questions; consent and ethics; a hands-on mock-interview round (you'll interview, be interviewed, and observe); and how to build an honest persona — with good and bad examples to compare.
