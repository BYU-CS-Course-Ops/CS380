## Purpose

This course is about a shift: from *doing assignments* to *founding a real project.* Instead of being handed a problem, you'll go find one worth solving, prove it's worth solving, and lay a foundation other people can build on.

Today sets that up. You'll see where the whole semester is headed — a launched, open-source project that you demo publicly at the end and that others could join and keep building — and you'll start the habit that feeds everything else: **noticing problems worth solving.**

---

## Learning objectives

By the end of this session, you'll be able to:

1. **Explain how this course is different** from your prior CS coursework — the founder/maintainer mindset.
2. **Describe where the semester is headed** and the path that gets you there.
3. **Name what makes a strong project** and tell a strong idea from a weak one.
4. **Explain divergent vs. convergent thinking** — and why we start by exploring widely.
5. **Recognize the course's learning outcomes** — including that clear technical communication runs through almost everything you'll produce.
6. **Start your idea journal** and understand what the first assignment (Idea Briefs) asks for.

## What we'll do together
