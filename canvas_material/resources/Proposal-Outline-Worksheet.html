Put a star next to it in Part 1. That is where your peer reviewers start — and where a skeptical reader will too.

Next (6b) — trade with a neighbor

  1. Which section is weakest? Name it in criteria language — "the users aren't bounded," not "it's vague."
  2. Which claim most needs evidence? Point at one line and ask "how do you know?"
  3. Do you believe the five-week arithmetic? Yes or no, and why.

Authors: capture, don't defend. Write down what you hear; decide later what to act on.
