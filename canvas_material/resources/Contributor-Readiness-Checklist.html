Every newcomer to any project asks the same five questions, whether it's a new hire's first week on a team, a first-time contributor to an open-source library, or you, eight months from now, reopening your own code. A project that answers them on purpose gets joined. One that answers them by accident mostly gets left.
As a self-check: mark your own project honestly. Every stall or fail goes on your launch punch list.
As a test script: a tester who has never seen the project works through it cold, from the repository alone, and marks what happened.
