# Context notes

Short instructor-only briefs on things **the students already know and I don't** —
usually because it was taught in a prerequisite I have never taught or taken.

Each note answers the same three questions:

1. **Where did they get it?** The course, the assigned reading, roughly when.
2. **What do they actually own?** What they read vs. what they built — those differ,
   and the built version is the one they'll reach for.
3. **How do I use it in CS 301R?** The lever it gives me, and where leaning on it
   would send a team the wrong way.

Not student-facing, not deployed. The prerequisite for CS 301R is **CS 340**, and
most students will also have taken **CS 240**; their repos are the source material:
`~/repos/softwaredesign` (CS 340) and `~/repos/softwareconstruction` (CS 240).
