## How the 14-week design maps onto real dates

The v3 design assumes 14 clean weeks of **Monday (Session A, lecture) + Wednesday (Session B, studio)**. The real Fall 2026 calendar breaks that pattern in three places, so the content is remapped (not renumbered away) as follows:

1. **The opening two Wednesdays (Sep 2 & Sep 9) absorb Design Week 1.** Because the term opens on a Wednesday and the very next Monday is Labor Day, the first two sessions are *both* isolated Wednesdays. They carry Week 1's two halves — course/founder-mindset overview, then the divergent problem-space workshop. Spreading Week 1 across two Wednesdays a week apart is a mild plus: students get extra incubation time for early idea generation.

2. **Design Weeks 2–14 map 1:1 onto the 13 full calendar weeks** from Sep 14 through Dec 9. The normal "Monday lecture / Wednesday studio" rhythm resumes at Design Week 2 and runs uninterrupted.

3. **Design Week 12 loses its Wednesday** to the Thanksgiving "No Classes" day (Nov 25). Its Monday lecture (contributor-readiness) is kept; the Wednesday peer-onboarding/studio work folds into Week 13's studio time (Week 11 already ran a repo audit + onboarding test, so this is the lowest-cost session to drop).

**Net result:** 27 held sessions vs. the design's 28 — one session absorbed at Thanksgiving, no checkpoint lost. Design week numbers are preserved throughout for cross-reference with v3.

## Winter comparison note (if the course were run in Winter 2027 instead)

BYU's Winter calendar shifts the same 14-week design in a few schedule-relevant ways. For reference, **Winter 2027** runs **Wed, Jan 6 – Wed, Apr 14**, finals **Apr 16–21**:

- **Same mid-week Wednesday start.** Winter 2027 also opens on a **Wednesday (Jan 6)**, so the "first session is a lone Wednesday" issue recurs — but only once, not twice.
- **MLK Day (Mon, Jan 18)** kills the second Monday, much like Labor Day does in Fall. So Winter's first two weeks are similarly Monday-light, and Design Week 1 again naturally spreads across the opening sessions.
- **"Monday Instruction" day (Tue, Feb 16).** After Presidents Day (Mon, Feb 15, no class), BYU runs a **Monday schedule on Tuesday Feb 16** to recover the lost Monday. A M/W course therefore *gains back* its Presidents-Day Monday — net Monday loss is zero, unlike Fall where Labor Day is simply gone. This makes Winter slightly richer in Session-A (lecture) time.
- **Spring "No Classes" day (Fri, Mar 19).** A Friday, so **no impact** on a M/W course (Fall's lost day, by contrast, is a Wednesday).
- **No Thanksgiving gap.** Winter has no equivalent mid-semester two-day class cancellation on a M/W day, so Design Week 12 keeps both sessions — Winter would run the **full 28 sessions** rather than Fall's 27.

**Bottom line:** Winter is marginally *more* forgiving for this M/W design — it preserves an extra Monday (via Feb 16 makeup) and loses no M/W session to a major break, so the only real adaptation is the single Wednesday-start week. Fall 2026's distinctive constraints are the double-Wednesday opening (Sep 2 + Sep 9) and the lost Thanksgiving Wednesday.
