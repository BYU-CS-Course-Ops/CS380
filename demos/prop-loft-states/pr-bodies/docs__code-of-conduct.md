**Problem.** No code of conduct, so a newcomer can't tell what's expected or how to report a problem.

**Approach.** Adopt the Contributor Covenant 3.0 rather than write our own. Only the two places the Covenant asks a project to fill in are changed: how to report (email the maintainer, or GitHub's reporting tools when the report is about the maintainer), and how enforcement works here (the Covenant's ladder, as written).

**Where to look first.** *Reporting an Issue* and the first paragraph of *Addressing and Repairing Harm*. Everything else is the Covenant's text.

**How you tested it.** Diffed against the Covenant's published text: two changed passages, front matter removed.
