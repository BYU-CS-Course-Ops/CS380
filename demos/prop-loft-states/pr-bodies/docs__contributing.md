**Problem.** No contributing guide, so a newcomer doesn't know where to start, or whether they're welcome.

**Approach.** `CONTRIBUTING.md` leads with welcome and a five-step first contribution, then other ways to help, the short form of the git standards, what to expect from review, the labels, and why the project is Apache-2.0 rather than MIT or the GPL. Policy comes last on purpose.

**Where to look first.** *Your first contribution*, then *Licensing your contribution*, which records the license rationale.

**How you tested it.** Followed every link; read it as someone who has never seen the project.
