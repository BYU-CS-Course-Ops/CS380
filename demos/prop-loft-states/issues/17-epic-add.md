### Goal

A non-technical volunteer can add an item, with a photo, from a phone, with no verbal instruction.

### Context

Design §2, goal 3; §4, Web UI and Image store; §6, *Downscale photos in the browser before upload*; §7, the HEIC risk and the risk that volunteers keep using the paper notebook.

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A volunteer who hasn't been shown how adds an item with a photo on their own phone.
- [ ] Photos from an iPhone (HEIC) and an Android phone both display.
- [ ] Every sub-issue is closed.

**In:** the add-item form, photo upload. **Out:** editing and deleting items (the admin covers those for now).
