### Goal

An item can have a photo, taken on a phone, that loads fast on the shop floor.

### Context

Design §4, Image store; §6, *Downscale photos in the browser before upload*; the upload spike in the proposal. Part of #{{E3}}. Needs #{{ADD}}.

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The add-item form takes a photo; the browser downscales it before upload.
- [ ] Photos are saved through the storage interface, not straight to disk.
- [ ] The item page and the item list show the photo.
