### Goal

Every checked-out item shows who has it, for which show, and when it's due back, and the wardrobe lead can see what's overdue.

### Context

Design §2, goal 2; §4, Checkout (at most one open checkout per item; an item is overdue when its due date has passed with no return); §4, *The failure case*.

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A volunteer checks an item out to an actor for a show, with a due date, from the item's page.
- [ ] The item list and the item page show what's out, to whom, and until when.
- [ ] A return closes the checkout; the overdue list shows what's past due.
- [ ] Every sub-issue is closed.

**In:** checkouts, returns, the overdue list. **Out:** reminders to actors (a non-goal: reminding stays a person's job).
