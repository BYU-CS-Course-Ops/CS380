### The problem

At a fitting, a volunteer needs to know whether we own something ("a men's waistcoat, size M?") while the actor is standing there. Today the catalog is one long list, and with 60 items on a phone that means scrolling and guessing.

### Who has this problem

Wardrobe volunteers, on a phone, on the shop floor.

### What you do today instead

Scroll the full list, or walk to the bins and look.

### An idea for how it could work (optional)

A search box at the top of the item list that matches on name, with filters for category and size. The design's goal is "do we own this?" answered in under a minute from a phone (design §2), so this is the next slice.
