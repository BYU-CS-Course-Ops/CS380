### Goal

A volunteer can do what volunteers need and nothing they shouldn't, and every page works on a phone held in one hand.

### Context

Design §4, Auth (two roles: wardrobe lead and volunteer); design §8, milestone 4. On the [roadmap](https://github.com/prop-loft/prop-loft/blob/main/docs/roadmap.md) after the MVP milestones: it isn't broken down yet, on purpose. It gets sub-issues when it's next.

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Volunteers can search, view, check out, return, and add; only the wardrobe lead can see the overdue list and change bins.
- [ ] Every page passes a one-handed phone walkthrough.
- [ ] Every sub-issue is closed.
